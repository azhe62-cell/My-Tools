All-in-One Free Online Tools

Toolmetri is a free, browser-based toolkit built for everyday digital work — from generating QR codes and passwords to calculating loans, converting units, and formatting code. Every tool runs instantly in your browser, with nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

We built Toolmetri because most "free tool" websites bury a simple task behind ads, forced accounts, or watered-down features. Our goal is the opposite: fast, private, no-friction utilities that just work.

Why Use Toolmetri

Most everyday digital tasks — resizing an image, checking a password's strength, converting a currency, calculating a loan's monthly payment — shouldn't require downloading software or creating an account. Toolmetri consolidates 25 of these small but essential utilities into a single, fast website so you can get the answer or the file you need and move on with your day.

Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your own browser using client-side JavaScript. That means your text, images, and numbers are never uploaded to a server. Nothing is stored, logged, or shared — your data stays on your device from start to finish, which matters if you're working with anything even mildly sensitive, like a password or a personal document.

We also keep the experience deliberately simple. No mandatory sign-up walls, no paywalled "pro" versions of basic features, and no artificial daily-use limits. Whether you use a tool once a year or a dozen times a day, it behaves the same way.

Toolmetri is actively maintained — new tools and blog guides are added regularly based on what people actually search for and need, and existing tools are refined as we get feedback from real users.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Toolmetri really free to use?

Yes. Every tool on Toolmetri is completely free, with no hidden fees, no premium tier, and no usage caps. There is no account or credit card required for any tool.

Do I need to create an account?

No. None of the tools require sign-up, login, or an email address. You can open any tool and use it immediately.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Toolmetri's tools run client-side, meaning the processing happens directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your text, images, and files are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Can I use Toolmetri on my phone?

Yes. The site is fully responsive and every tool works on mobile browsers, including image tools like the resizer and compressor.

How many tools does Toolmetri have?

Toolmetri currently offers 25 tools spanning QR/barcode generation, calculators, unit and currency converters, image utilities, and developer formatting tools like JSON and Base64. New tools are added regularly.

Where can I learn more about a specific tool?

Visit the Blog, where we publish in-depth guides explaining how each tool works and when to use it.
