About Toolmetri

A free, browser-based toolkit built to make everyday digital tasks faster and simpler.

Our Story

Toolmetri started as a simple frustration: needing to generate a QR code, resize an image, or calculate a loan payment, and having to wade through ad-heavy websites that force a signup or hide the actual tool behind three pop-ups. We built Toolmetri as the alternative — a single site where common digital tasks are handled quickly, without friction.

What began as a handful of tools has grown into a suite of 25 utilities covering QR and barcode generation, everyday calculators, unit and currency conversion, image editing, and developer formatting tools like JSON and Base64. Each tool is built to be self-contained, fast, and genuinely free — not a limited "trial" version of a paid product.

Toolmetri is actively maintained and expanded. We add new tools based on what people search for and struggle to find elsewhere, and we publish in-depth blog guides explaining how to get the most out of each one.

What We Believe

Privacy First

Every tool runs client-side in your browser. Your data is never uploaded to our servers or stored anywhere.

No Friction

No accounts, no email verification, no forced downloads. Open a tool and use it immediately.

Genuinely Free

No artificial daily limits or paywalled "pro" features. What you see is what you get, for everyone.

Have a Suggestion?

If there's a tool you wish existed, or you've spotted something that isn't working right, we'd like to hear about it.
