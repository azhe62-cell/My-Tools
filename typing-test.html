About This Typing Test

This free typing test measures your typing speed in words per minute (WPM) and your accuracy as you type. Choose a test duration from 15 seconds up to 5 minutes, and a difficulty level from easy everyday words to more complex vocabulary. The test runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and no account is required. Your personal best for each duration and difficulty combination is saved on your own device so you can track your progress over time.
